# Morningside Heights street trees (NYC 2015 Street Tree Census)

Filtering the census to the Morningside Heights neighborhood tabulation area (MN09) gives 2,751 records (2,642 living trees, 62 dead, 47 stumps), with no duplicate tree IDs, but the neighborhood tag is loose: 22 rows carry the Central Park zip code 83 and 193 sit south of roughly 110th Street, and the 109 dead or stump rows have no species or health value by design. The five most common species among living trees are honeylocust (391), ginkgo (371), American elm (296), Callery pear (232) and pin oak (225), which together make up 57% of the 2,642 living trees. Overall 78.4% of living trees (2,071) are rated in Good health, with 16.5% Fair and 5.1% Poor, or 75.3% if the dead and stump rows are counted against the total. Health varies sharply by species, from 91% Good for honeylocust and 87% for American elm down to 66% for ginkgo, the second most common tree on the list. Dropping the 193 southernmost rows swaps honeylocust and ginkgo at the top but leaves the same five species and a Good share of 77.7%, though these are one-time 2015 ratings, mostly made by volunteers (1,881 of the 2,751 records), so the health shares are best read as approximate.
